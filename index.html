About

I'm Aarav, a video editor working across narrative, commercial, and social formats. My focus is pacing — cutting so the story reads clearly on the first watch, whether that's a 15-second ad or a 20-minute short film.

I've spent the last few years cutting for independent filmmakers and small brands, learning to move fast without losing craft. Every project starts the same way: understand what the footage is trying to say, then get out of its way.

  • SoftwarePremiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, After Effects
  • Based inJaipur, Rajasthan — available remotely
  • TurnaroundTypically 3–5 days for short-form work
